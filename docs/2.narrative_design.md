## Epic
As a mobile gamer who wants a simple and personalised way to discover new games, I want a mobile application that lets me browse, search, and explore curated game information from the RAWG.io database through an intuitive and well‑designed interface, so that I can quickly find games that match my interests, access reliable information, and enjoy a smooth, ethical, and trustworthy discovery experience without being overwhelmed by large, unfiltered datasets.

## Personas
For our Personas we chose two very different end users to target our application towards, showing that the program can be used by a wide variety of users, no matter their experience levels in gaming. 

Our first Persona is Joe, a 28-year-old marketing assistant living in surrey. Joe leads a busy work life and therefore only plays games casually when he has the time so usually about once/twice a week. Joe enjoys gaming on multiple platforms including on his Nintendo Switch due to the convenience of being able to travel with it and on his PC when he has more time. Due to his busy work schedule, Joe doesn’t follow gaming news closely and so he often feels out of the loop when trying to find new games to play. At work Joe uses a lot of different kinds of software and he always prefers using apps that are simple to use and which have clean user interfaces   
like his most used app Notion. He is less likely to engage with applications which lack easy to use interfaces and he values quick navigation across pages due to his limited free time.

## Scenario

Scenario 1: (Casual user)

- Joe is a marketing manager who leads an intense life, which leaves him little time to engage in leisurely activities. While he sometimes enjoys playing mobile games for fun, he does not participate in the gaming community and is not aware of recent game releases.
Whenever Joe decides to play a new game, he ends up frustrated due to most apps providing a huge list of games in an unfiltered manner. 
One day, Joe opens the app and finds a well designed UI, which recommends games for him based on his interaction with the platform.
In addition, he looks into games that are currently featured, including those that are newly released and on sale
Joe then searches for games and selects a particular one by clicking on it. On doing so, he discovers more information about the selected game, which includes its description and ratings available through the RAWG.io database.
After some time, Joe likes a particular game and marks it as his favorite. Then he downloads it when he has free time in the future.
Therefore, Joe manages to find a good game for himself despite having a busy schedule and not being part of the gaming community.

## User Stories

As a casual gamer, I want to be able to find games by name or genre, so that I can find games that match my interest.

As a user, I want to view detailed information about a game (description, ratings, release date), so that I can decide whether it’s worth playing.

As a user, I want to save games to a favourites list, so that I can easily return to them later.

As a user, I want to see featured games such as discounts and new releases, so that I can discover trending or good-value games.

As an experienced gamer, I want to filter and compare games using detailed metadata (such as ratings, genres, platforms, and release dates), so that I can efficiently evaluate and select games that match my specific preferences and standards.
